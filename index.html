Fishing and Recreational Angling

Explores recreational fishing in Australia, focusing on fishing locations, catch
patterns, seasonal changes, motivations, fishing methods, and expenditure. It provides an overview of recreational fishing activities and trends across Australia.

Where Australians Fish

Covers fishing participation across Australia, catch differences across Queensland regions,
and fishing locations in Victoria.

What Australians Catch

Explores differences in fish harvest across states and species,
as well as differences between released and kept catches.

Fishing Activity and Catch Patterns

Covers catch by state and species, released versus kept catches,
fishing effort by waterbody, seasonal harvest patterns,
and changes in fishing days over time.

Why We Fish and What It Costs

Covers people's reasons for fishing, the fishing methods they use,
and their average expenses.

Overall, the charts reveal different recreational fishing patterns across Australia.
Showing participation rates, fishing locations, catch differences, and seasonal changes.
Also highlighting people's motivations, fishing methods, and associated costs.
Together, these findings provide clearer understanding of Australia's recreational fishing.
